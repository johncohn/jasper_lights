# Jasper Lights: User Guide

## Buttons

Hold the M5 with the screen facing you and the writing upright. The big button under the screen is the **M5 button**; the two side buttons are **Top** and **Bottom**.

| Button | Press | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| **M5** (big front button) | Next pattern (switches to manual) | **1.5 s**: back to auto · **3.5 s**: turn off |
| **Top** | Brightness (6 levels, wraps around) | – |
| **Bottom** | Speed (6 levels, wraps around) | **1 s**: black & white mode on/off |

While you hold the M5 button, the screen tells you what letting go will do.

## Modes

- **Auto** (normal): a new pattern every minute. The lights always start in auto.
- **Manual**: press the M5 button to step through patterns; the lights stay on the one you pick. Hold 1.5 s to go back to auto.
- **Black & white**: only the black & white patterns are shown, in auto or manual. The screen turns black and says *Jasper B&W*.

## Turning off and on

- **Off**: hold the M5 button for 3.5 s, then let go. The lights and screen go dark.
- **Auto-off**: after an hour with no button presses it turns off by itself.
- **On**: press the M5 button.

Don't hold the Bottom button for 6 s or more: it is also the M5's power switch, so that turns the M5 off completely, and it has to be switched on again with the Bottom button.

## Screen

- Top line: *Jasper Lights* (or *Jasper B&W*), and **AUTO** or **MAN**
- The pattern's number and name
- **Bright** and **Speed**: one box per level, filled up to the current one

## Remembered settings

Brightness, speed, black & white mode and the current pattern are remembered when it's turned off or loses power.
